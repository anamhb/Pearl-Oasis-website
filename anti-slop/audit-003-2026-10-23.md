## Findings

### 19. Control bar falls below the first view (MEDIUM, R-03)

At 320x800 the control pill starts at `798.2px` in a `800px` viewport, so only its top edge is visible. At 320x640 it is far below the fold. At 1024x768 the pill runs from `728.8px` to roughly `790px`, so the bottom is cut by 22px; the previous/next buttons and the counter are still visible and their centres are clickable, but the pill is not fully visible. The 390, 768 and 1440 viewports show the pill fully.

Evidence from the rendered page (all widths):

| Viewport | Controls top | Controls bottom | Fully in view |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 320x800 | 798.2 | 860.2 | no |
| 390x844 | 672.4 | 734.4 | yes |
| 768x1024 | 712.4 | 774.4 | yes |
| 1024x768 | 728.8 | 790.8 | no |
| 1440x900 | 712.4 | 774.4 | yes |

The buttons are 44x44 and work. The 320 user can scroll or swipe; the 1024 user can click the visible upper part of the buttons. The finding is about first-view usability, not a broken control.

Proposed fix: at the narrow end, reduce the base section padding, heading margin and stage padding slightly (`py-14` to `py-10`, `mb-8` to `mb-6`, controls `mt-8` to `mt-6`, stage `padding-block` to `0.75rem`), and consider the same at the 1024 breakpoint. Re-measure the pill position at 320x800, 320x640 and 1024x768. Do not shrink the card text or clip the quotes.

### 20. Side-card visible target is narrower than 44px at 320/390 (MEDIUM, R-03)

The active card spans most of the viewport, so the visible side-card peeks are the gutters between the active card and the screen edges. At 320 the active card is 262px wide with 29px gutters; at 390 the gutters are 35px. The overlay button covers the whole side card, but only the 29px/35px strip is visible and hittable. A 29px pointer target is below the 44px minimum even though its height is the full card.

Evidence: at 320x800 the measured visible width outside the active card was `0.125` of the 231px side card (`29px`), and a real pointer click at `x=306, y=433.5` hit `Show testimonial 2 of 5`, moved to index 1 and focused the stage. At 390 the same fraction was `0.125` of a wider card (`35px`).

Proposed fix: narrow the active card slightly at base widths (for example `82vw` to `78vw`) to widen the peeks, or move the side-card action to a dedicated 44px edge control outside the card. Keep the 44px previous/next controls and swipe as the primary paths.

### 21. Desktop side cards are nearly fully exposed (LOW, craft)

At 1024 and 1440 the side cards are fully inside the stage and only 18.2% of each card is behind the active card. The side card's visible width outside the active card is 81.8% at both desktop widths. The cards are scaled to 0.88, dimmed to `brightness(0.8)` and carry no gold border, so the active card still reads as the focus. The overlap itself is moderate: 78% shift and 0.88 scale leave the side cards large.

Proposed fix: if the owner wants a tighter centre focus, reduce the shift from `78%` to about `68%` (about 70% of each side card visible) or reduce the side scale to about `0.84`. Re-check that the side cards remain readable and clickable at 768 and 320. This is a visual direction choice, not a defect.

### 22. Stage-wide `user-select: none` blocks deliberate copying (LOW, craft)

`.testimonial-carousel__stage` sets `user-select: none`. The brief asked to prevent accidental text selection during swipes. The current rule also prevents a mouse or keyboard user from selecting the active quote on purpose. The active quote is placeholder text, so the practical impact is small, but the protection is broader than the interaction it was meant to guard.

Proposed fix: allow selection in the active card on pointer devices, or add a temporary selection guard only while a horizontal touch swipe is in progress, and keep `touch-action: pan-y` on the stage. Re-run the touch and text-selection checks.

### 23. Eyebrow wraps to two lines at 320 (LOW, craft/R-06)

The eyebrow uses `text-[11px] tracking-[0.2em]` at base and `sm:text-xs sm:tracking-[0.3em]` above 640px. At 320 the full label is 33px tall, which means two lines. At 390 it is 16.5px and at 768 and above 16px, so one line. The 11px size is smaller than the 12px body note and is the tightest type in the section.

Proposed fix: either accept a deliberate two-line eyebrow at 320 with a slightly reduced line-height, or use `10px` and `0.16em` tracking at base so the label fits one line. Re-check the heading rhythm and the control position if the heading block changes height.

### 24. Side-card meta is close to the AA floor after filtering (LOW, R-25)

The active meta measures 7.36:1 on theme A and 6.68:1 on theme B. The side cards apply `saturate(0.65) brightness(0.8)`, which brings the 12px meta to 4.99:1 (A) and 4.66:1 (B). Both pass 4.5:1, but the warm theme has only 0.16 of margin. The quote text and publication remain at 9.6:1 or better.

Proposed fix: raise the side brightness to `0.82` (4.88:1 and 5.23:1) or use a lighter meta color on theme B. Re-measure the actual composited card, not the unfiltered token.

### 25. Side-card focus also picks up the gold hover border (LOW, craft)

Keyboard focus on a side action brings the card to full brightness and color through the `:has(:focus-visible)` rule. The same card can also still match `:hover` from the pointer, which gives it a `0.4` alpha gold border. The active card uses `0.5` alpha gold, so focused and active cards can look close. The 2px inset focus ring is the clear differentiator.

Proposed fix: keep the side-card border neutral on focus and rely on the focus ring, or give focus a distinct treatment from both the hover and active states. Verify with the pointer parked away from the card.

### 26. Side-card accessible names are positions only (LOW, craft/WCAG 2.5.3)

The side buttons are labelled "Show testimonial 5 of 5" and "Show testimonial 2 of 5". The active card exposes its publication and placeholder context, but the side action gives no indication of what it will show beyond the index. A screen reader user can read the article content, but the button name itself is generic.

Proposed fix: add an `aria-describedby` pointing at the card's publication or a short hidden description, or include the publication in the action name. Keep the visible card aware of the action. Re-check the tab order and the live announcement.

### 27. Heading scrim reads as a rectangle over the film (LOW, craft)

The heading sits on a local radial scrim inside `inset-x-0 -inset-y-14`. At 1024 and 1440 the scrim is a visible dark rectangle behind the heading, with the bright film around it. It does its contrast job (the earlier heading measurements are 16.4:1 for the h2, about 8:1 for the eyebrow and 11.2:1 for the note), but the shape is more panel-like than blended.

Proposed fix: soften the gradient edge or use a wider, lower-opacity linear scrim that follows the section width. Re-measure worst-pixel contrast over bright frames before reducing the opacity.

### 28. Counter slash is below AA (LOW, R-25)

The counter is inside an `aria-hidden` wrapper and the live region provides the same information. The visible `01` is gold, the `05` is `neutral-400` (about 7.4:1), and the `/` is `neutral-600` (`#525252` on `#0a0b0d`, roughly 2.6:1). The slash is a separator, but it is still visible text.

Proposed fix: use `neutral-400` for the slash or replace it with a non-text separator. The live region already carries the position, so no announcement change is needed.
