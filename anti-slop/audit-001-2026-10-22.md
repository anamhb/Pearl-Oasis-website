# Anti-Slop Audit 001

- Date: 2026-10-22
- Project: Pearl Oasis V1
- Mode: AFTER (session override), audit only, no UI changes made
- Build audited: `dist/assets/index-BYQ3y0-l.js` on `http://localhost:4173`, current working tree
- Viewports: 320x568, 390x844, 768x1024, 1440x900 (headless Chrome via CDP)
- Scope: the rendered page and the source that produces it

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW. Findings marked "craft" have no numbered rule and are prioritized by severity.

### Context from the previous pass

An earlier anti-slop pass on this project is recorded in `changes/anti-slop-approved-findings.md` (approved findings 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 13, 15, 22). That pass fixed the fabricated press quotes (now labeled placeholders), footer and nav target heights, the full-menu dialog keyboard behavior, hidden menu CTAs, reduced-motion handling for the crest and CSS loops, and the meta description. Its finding 1 (em dashes) was deliberately not approved. This report uses its own numbering. Where a previous decision applies, the finding says so, and the owner can keep that decision.

## Findings

### 1. Em dash in shipped text (HIGH, R-02)

R-02 bans the em dash character in any text. Two places ship it:

- `index.html:6` title and `index.html:8` `og:title`: the title joins "Pearl Oasis" and "Immersive 3D Restaurant" with an em dash. Confirmed in the live DOM (`document.title`) at every viewport.
- `src/components/sections/WelcomeStory.jsx:456`: "Every great meal carries an essence" is followed by an em dash before "the subtle aroma of spices, the richness of texture...". Visible in the 390px story screenshot.

This was finding 1 in the previous pass and was not approved. It is re-raised only because it still ships and R-02 is a Hard Gate. Declining it again is a valid owner decision, but it should be recorded as a deliberate override.

Fix: title `Pearl Oasis: Immersive 3D Restaurant`; rewrite the story sentence with a comma or full stop. The en dashes in the opening hours are range markers, not the banned character, so they can stay.

### 2. Tap targets below 44px (HIGH, R-03)

Measured live with the layout in its resting state:

- `Enter Restaurant`, 198x37 at 320 and 390 (`EntranceExperience.jsx:729`, `px-6 py-2.5`, 11px label).
- `UNFOLD THE STORY`, 128x42 at 320 and 390 (`EntranceExperience.jsx:743-746`, 9px label).
- Header `VIEW MENU`, 134x36 at 768 (`Navigation.jsx:64`, `py-2.5`).
- Footer links, 31x44 for `Menu` (`Footer.jsx:60-75`): height is fine, width follows the text.

Fix: give each control a 44px hit height (`py-3` or `min-h-11`) and the footer links `min-w-11` or a block-level row. Re-measure at 320/390/768.

### 3. Testimonials without real reviews (HIGH, R-18)

`Testimonials.jsx:141-185` ships five placeholder cards; the live card reads "Sample guest feedback placeholder... Until then, this card stands in for a genuine review" with the meta "Guest Review Placeholder / Not a real review". The labeling satisfies R-38. The previous pass approved turning fabricated quotes into these labeled placeholders, so the current state is a deliberate, honest choice. R-18 is still explicit: if there are no real testimonials, do not create the section. This audit does not re-open the approved fix; it flags that the section still lacks real content.

Decision for the owner: keep the labeled placeholders (recorded as an override), or remove the section and the "Testimonial" nav item at launch until real, verifiable quotes exist. Do not invent quotes to fill it.

### 4. Hero eyebrow contrast over the sequence (HIGH, R-25)

`EntranceExperience.jsx:712` sets the eyebrow to `#fed766` at 10px, over the center scrim at 0.36 (`:677-678`). At 50% of the hero scroll (frame 148 at 390px, copy still at opacity 1) the wall behind the eyebrow samples `#bca482`, `#9c8b6d`, `#8b7a5d`, and up to `#d9cea6`. Contrast against `#fed766` is 1.13 to 3.00:1; 10px text needs 4.5:1. The 390 screenshot shows the label nearly vanishing over the bright wall. The capture already includes the label's drop shadow, so the untouched frame is no darker.

The H1 and lead in the same frame sit over the dark shelf/table and pass (measured 6.5:1 and 15.5:1 against sampled pixels).

Fix: darken the local band behind the copy (about 0.65 alpha at the eyebrow's line, not the whole frame) or set the eyebrow to cream with a stronger shadow, then re-measure at the mid-scroll frames where the copy is still opaque. The frames themselves do not need to change.

### 5. Invisible focusable control, and Escape does not close the menu (HIGH, R-32)

- `EntranceExperience.jsx:757-760`: the dining copy starts at `opacity: 0` with `pointer-events: none`, but no `inert`, `aria-hidden`, or `tabIndex`. In the live tab order it is stop 5 of 14 at 390 and stop 9 at 1440, at opacity 0. At the end of the hero the entrance copy (including `Enter Restaurant`) is invisible and still tabbable, so there are two ghost stops depending on the phase.
- `Navigation.jsx:69-80`: the mobile menu toggle has no Escape handler. Opening it and pressing Escape leaves `aria-expanded="true"`.

Fix: drive `inert` / `tabIndex={-1}` / `aria-hidden` from the same mix value that drives opacity, or reduce the hero to one CTA. Add a `keydown` listener for Escape that closes the menu and returns focus to the toggle.

### 6. Unsourced halal claim beside placeholder details (HIGH, R-36)

`MenuExperience.jsx:696`: "100% Zabiha Halal • Freshly prepared to order in Kohat". The repo has no source for the certification. The previous pass recorded that the owner has verified the address and hours, but the footer still tells visitors those details "are editable placeholders", which is now false. A compliance-style claim and a stale placeholder note undermine each other.

Fix: record the owner's confirmation (or the certifying body) for the halal claim, or remove it. Remove or update the footer note so it matches the verified state. This is a content confirmation, not a design change.

### 7. Endless dish loop (MEDIUM, R-19)

`SignatureDishes.jsx:266-274` runs a continuous requestAnimationFrame loop: seven dishes travel in, rest, and exit forever. The rings add 50s, 38s, and 75s infinite spins (`index.css:105-115`). The only pause is `onMouseEnter` (`SignatureDishes.jsx:369-376`), which does not exist on touch. The reduced-motion still is handled.

Fix: run the entrance once and settle the constellation, restart on hover, tap, or focus, or stop after one cycle. Keep the still frame for reduced motion.

### 8. Sparkle capsule badge (MEDIUM, R-04 with R-09)

`MenuExperience.jsx:371-374` (desktop) and `:544-545` (mobile): a `rounded-full` badge with a gold border, 10px uppercase tracking, and a Lucide `Sparkles` icon above "Our Menu". The sparkle is the generic AI glyph and has no relation to "Kohat Culinary Collection". The badge also stacks capsule, border, uppercase, and icon, the combination R-09 warns about.

Fix: remove the sparkle, or remove the badge and let the heading carry the section.

### 9. Backdrop blur with no visible effect (MEDIUM, R-10)

Ten revealed menu cards use `backdrop-blur-md` (`MenuExperience.jsx:421`, `:469`) plus the modal overlay (`:623`). The cards are `bg-[#121419]/95`, so the blur is invisible; the page still pays for 11 blurred layers.

Fix: drop the blur from the cards and keep at most one blurred surface where it is actually perceptible.

### 10. The "full menu" is not a menu (MEDIUM, craft C-2)

`MenuExperience.jsx:653-687`: the dialog repeats the same 10 category cards with no dishes and no prices, under the title "Complete Culinary Catalog". The 10 cards are `line-clamp-1` there and `line-clamp-1` in the section; at 320 every description is clipped, and the full sentence is available nowhere. Live check: 10 cards, 0 prices.

Fix: either supply the real menu content (owner decision, with prices if they are public), or rename the CTA and title to "Menu Sections" and let descriptions wrap so nothing is lost.

### 11. CTA wording (LOW, R-15)

- `Explore Full Menu` (`MenuExperience.jsx:524`, `:611`) uses "Explore", which R-15 lists as a default CTA.
- `Reserve Table & Order` (`:710`) is a `tel:` link: it calls, it cannot reserve or order in the page.

Fix: "See the Full Menu" and "Call to Reserve" (or "Call to Reserve & Order"). Both remain specific and honest.

### 12. Copy cluster and a typo (LOW, R-16)

`WelcomeStory.jsx:471` reads "‘Pearl Oasis’ unveils a vibrant tapestry of flavors in Kohat: an exceptional fusion... served with devoted precision to every detail..." with a forced rule of three at `:477`. This is the copy skill's own AI cluster ("vibrant tapestry", "exceptional fusion", three-part closer). The hero and footer share the register ("refined gastronomy", "timeless hospitality"), but the story paragraph is the dense case.

Also `MenuExperience.jsx:130` and the visible card and modal title read "Chief Special Pizza Section"; it should be "Chef's Special".

Fix: rewrite the story paragraph with plain, owner-supplied specifics (no invented facts) and correct the title.

### 13. Radius language (LOW, R-11)

4px on the hero CTA and the header CTA, a full pill on the menu CTA, 8px on the modal buttons, 12 to 16px on cards, a circle for the icon-only close. No rule is broken by any one radius, but there is no stated hierarchy behind the set.

Fix: pick one button radius and one card radius, apply them to every instance, and keep the circle only for icon-only controls.

### 14. Smooth scrolling ignores reduced motion (LOW, craft C-4)

`index.html` puts `scroll-smooth` on `<html>` and `Footer.jsx:6-8` uses `behavior: 'smooth'`. The reduced-motion block (`index.css:119-128`) only removes CSS animations, so anchor jumps and Return to Top still glide.

Fix: set `scroll-behavior: auto` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and choose the scroll behavior from the same media query in the footer handler.

### 15. False affordances (LOW, craft C-2)

- The seven dish images carry `cursor-pointer` and `hover:scale-105` (`SignatureDishes.jsx:388`) with no click or link; hover is also the only pause.
- The 10 menu cards show a gold hover border (`MenuExperience.jsx:421`, `:469`) but have no action. Touch users never see the hover and nothing happens on tap.

Fix: remove the pointer cursor, the scale, and the hover border, or make them real (cards open the matching section, dishes open a detail view).

### 16. Hero cue: accessible name does not contain the visible label (MEDIUM, WCAG 2.5.3, craft)

`EntranceExperience.jsx:741-744`: the button shows "UNFOLD THE STORY" but its `aria-label` is "Scroll to Our Story". Speech-recognition users asking for the visible label cannot activate it, and the accessible name and visible label describe the same action with different words. The previous pass logged this as a Lighthouse `label-content-name-mismatch` item and left it unapproved.

Fix: `aria-label="Unfold the story: scroll to Our Story"` (the visible label first), or remove the `aria-label` and let the visible text name the button.

### 17. Footer heading order (LOW, R-20 hierarchy, craft)

The page runs h2 (section headings) and then jumps to h4 for "Explore", "Opening Hours", and "Sanctuary Location" in the footer (`Footer.jsx:55`, `:83`, `:114`). There is no h3 between them, so the document outline skips a level. The previous pass logged this as a Lighthouse `heading-order` item and left it unapproved.

Fix: use h2 or h3 for the footer column titles; keep the visual size unchanged.

## What passed

- Hero and Our Story sequences: frame 298 reached with `naturalWidth` 1920, no blank samples in the Phase 3 probe, and no console errors in this audit. Focus semantics are the only hero defect found.
- Static text contrast: every solid pairing checked passes AA, including footer muted text (7.25:1), menu descriptions (7.06:1), section intro (9.36:1), story body (16.41:1), and the gold button label (9.11:1).
- Layout: zero horizontal overflow at 320, 390, 768, and 1440; the menu switches to its mobile list below 768 and falls back on short viewports.
- Menu dialog: `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, labelled heading, focus moved to Close, trapped Tab, Escape closes, focus returns to the opener. Verified at 390 and 1440.
- Navigation: every `#` target resolves; the mobile links close the panel on tap; the menu toggle is 44x44.
- Images: all 40 have an `alt` attribute; the 10 decorative frame images use an empty `alt` with `aria-hidden`, correctly.
- Reduced motion: hero, story, crest, dish rings, and the testimonial video all stop; the smooth-scroll gap is finding 14.
- No theme toggle ships, so R-34 is not applicable. The dark-only palette has a legitimate fine-dining reason and fits R-21 and R-29 (charcoal, cream, gold).

## Observations, not findings

- No `DESIGN.md` exists. The direction is evident and approved (charcoal, cream, gold, Cormorant, Cinzel, Alex Brush), but it is not written in one place, which is what R-31 and R-37 ask for. Writing it would make future audits cheaper.
- The final hero frame is a chafer close-up under the "The Grand Dining Hall" label. The copy and the frame disagree at the very end, but changing it means changing artwork, which is out of scope for this audit.
- `ReservationSection.jsx`, `InteriorExperience.jsx`, and `utils/audio.js` are not imported by the app. Dead code, no user impact.

## Limitations

- Headless Chrome on Windows with emulated viewports and touch. No physical iPhone or Android, no Safari, no Firefox, no real screen reader.
- Contrast over the footage was sampled at the 50% hero frame and the story mid frame. Other frames can be brighter or darker; finding 4 is tied to the frames where the copy is still opaque, which is the state captured.
- The testimonial film is remote (Cloudinary); its offline or blocked behavior was not tested.
- The audit opened the menu dialog through the app's own handler because the mobile CTA sits below the fold during the pinned reveal. The dialog itself was exercised with real mouse clicks and Escape.
